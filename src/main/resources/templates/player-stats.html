+ What These Stats Mean

wRC+ compares total offensive value to league average. 100 = average. 120 = 20% better than league average.

WAR (Wins Above Replacement) estimates how many wins this player adds over a replacement-level player. 2+ is solid starter, 5+ is All-Star.

ISO (Isolated Power) = SLG minus AVG. Measures raw power. .200+ is elite. .150 is average.

Hard-Hit% is the percentage of batted balls hit at 95+ mph exit velocity. League average is ~38%.

Barrel% is the percentage of balls hit with optimal EV + launch angle (think "squared up" contact). League average is ~7%.

OAA (Outs Above Average) measures fielding range. Positive = better than average, negative = worse.

+ What These Stats Mean

FIP (Fielding Independent Pitching) measures what ERA should be based only on strikeouts, walks, and home runs. ERA can be affected by defense; FIP removes that noise.

xFIP is like FIP but also normalizes home run rate. It's a better indicator of future performance.

K-BB% is strikeout rate minus walk rate. Elite pitchers are 20%+. It's one of the best single indicators of pitching quality.

Whiff% is the percentage of swings that miss. Higher = better for pitchers. 30%+ is elite.

Hard-Hit% allowed: what percentage of balls put in play against this pitcher were hit 95+ mph. Lower is better for pitchers.

+ Stat Meanings

wRC+ Runs created per plate appearance, adjusted for ballpark and league. 100 is average, 120 is 20% better than average.

OPS On-base percentage plus slugging. A quick read on total offensive production.

ISO Slugging minus batting average. Isolates raw power from the ability to make contact.

FIP What a pitcher's ERA would be if his defence were average. Built from strikeouts, walks and home runs only, so it strips out luck and fielding.

K-BB% Strikeout rate minus walk rate. One of the most stable measures of a pitcher's command of an at-bat.

WHIP Walks and hits allowed per inning. How often a pitcher lets runners on.

BABIP Batting average on balls in play. Far above or below .300 usually signals luck that will not last.

Hard-hit % Share of batted balls hit 95 mph or harder.

Barrel % Share of batted balls hit at the exit velocity and launch angle combination that produces the most damage.

Outs Above Average Fielding runs saved versus an average defender at the same position, from Statcast tracking.

Defensive efficiency Share of balls in play a defence converts into outs.

Park factor How a ballpark changes run or home run scoring versus a neutral park. 100 is neutral, 106 means 6% more.

Leverage index How much the current moment matters to the outcome. 1.0 is an average situation, 2.0 or higher is a genuine turning point.
